UNIVERSITY OF THE WESTERN CAPE  STUDENT SUPPORT SERVICES DIRECTORY
1. Centre for Student Support Services (CSSS)
Overview:
The Centre for Student Support Services (CSSS) offers holistic student development and wellness support to enhance academic performance, personal growth, and social engagement. It serves as the central hub for non-academic student services.
Main Units within CSSS:
•	Academic Support & Development
•	Therapeutic Services (Counselling & Mental Health)
•	Disability Services
•	Leadership & Social Responsibility Office
•	Graduate and Student Success Programmes
Contact:

📧 csss@uwc.ac.za

2. Disability Services (within CSSS)
Overview:
Supports students with physical, sensory, learning, and psychological disabilities to ensure equal access to academic and campus life.
Key Services:
•	Assistive technology and accessible learning materials
•	Academic accommodations (e.g., extra time, accessible venues)
•	Orientation and awareness workshops
•	Advocacy for disability inclusion

3. Therapeutic Services (Counselling & Mental Health)
Overview:
Provides confidential psychological and emotional support to students facing stress, anxiety, or personal difficulties. The service promotes mental wellbeing and resilience.
Key Services:
•	Individual and group counselling
•	Crisis intervention
•	Mental health awareness and workshops
•	24/7 student helpline
•	Access to the Wysa wellbeing app (AI chatbot for mental health support)
